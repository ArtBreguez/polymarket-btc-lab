## Live Trader (v19) — Known Issues

### CRITICAL: Gate 4 (Execution Gate) NOT Integrated
- `check_execution_gate()` exists in data_quality_gate.py but is NEVER called in live_trader.py
- The win rate circuit breaker (<40% WR over 20 trades → pause) is completely bypassed
- Ask price sanity at execution level is also bypassed
- **Action needed**: Add gate.check_execution_gate(ask_price) before order placement

### SIGNIFICANT: Bot Not Trading (Edge Threshold Too High)
- MIN_EDGE = 0.10 (10%) is impossible to achieve with current market conditions
- Model confidence tops out at ~75%, typical asks are $0.62-$0.75
- Best edge observed: 5.9% (still 4.1% short of threshold)
- 100% of recent slots skipped
- **Math**: edge = conf - ask. With conf=75% and ask=0.72, edge=3%. To get 10% edge, need conf>82% (model never reaches this) OR ask<0.65 (rare)
- **Recommended fix**: Lower MIN_EDGE from 0.10 to 0.05, MIN_EDGE_MID from 0.05 to 0.03

### SIGNIFICANT: WS Price Cache Ineffective
- All ask prices show "HTTP" source (REST /book fallback) despite CLOB WS daemon running
- WS daemon keepalives at 30s with 68-70 tokens subscribed
- Prices consistently stale or missing from cache → adds 0.5-2s latency per REST call
